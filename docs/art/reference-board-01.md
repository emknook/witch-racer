# Witch Racer — reference board 01

**Purpose:** a first visual reference board for exploring Witch Racer's art direction. These images are references, not approved game assets or decisions. The project direction remains PSX-inspired low-poly 3D made in Blender; this board explores readable environments, track shapes, night lighting and witch-flight shorthand.

Images are embedded from their original Wikimedia Commons files rather than copied into this repository. Each pin links to its source and records the creator and license. CC BY images are reused unmodified and attributed; CC0 images are credited as a courtesy.

### Reading this board

**Possible direction to explore:** crisp low-poly silhouettes, visible track contours, atmospheric night lighting and controlled bright accents.

**Not decided here:** exact palette, texture filtering, fog, vertex wobble, lighting model, character clothing or final broom shapes. Those remain open in the [art direction notes](README.md).
