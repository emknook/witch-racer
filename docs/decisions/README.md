# Decision log

## Confirmed by project owner
- Engine: Godot, with latest stable version as ongoing target.
- Modeling: Blender; visual direction: PSX-inspired low-poly.
- Modes: singleplayer and local multiplayer; future online multiplayer should remain architecturally feasible.
- Eight established witch racers are planned, with blue, yellow, green, red, purple and mixed hair styles. The female story protagonist is separate; whether this makes her a ninth selectable racer remains open.
- Narrative: a friendly amateur beginning in the protagonist's shed, potentially with a friend; grassroots-to-international championship progression, not a mandatory school or club.
- Championship organizers secretly prepare successive generations of racers to survive and recapture Walpurgis. The reveal occurs around 70–80% of story-track progression.
- Finale: a large outer racing lap creates a magical boundary, smaller rune-shaped routes illuminate the sealing spell, and a final group race lures Walpurgis into its center. The spell's overhead UI lights completed paths; animated transitions accompany stages.
- All eight established witches participate in the final ritual; seven are AI-controlled in singleplayer.
- Walpurgis appears primarily in story sequences and special races; in special races she **chases from behind**, and being caught is dangerous. Recorded player ghost trajectories can inform her pursuit speed and path.
- Racing format: F-Zero-inspired, high-speed arcade racing on defined tracks; no unrestricted free flight. Banking, loops, and vertical elements are design directions to prototype.
- Repository is documentation source of truth; use structured Markdown and readable, prose-like code.

## Proposed, not yet approved
- Recovered Walpurgisnacht details such as Wardens and thirteen seals remain working lore, not fully approved canon. The Grand Broom Prix placeholder is superseded.
- A friend who may become a mechanic/racer and a coach/scout who notices the protagonist are suggested supporting roles, not confirmed character assignments.
- Eight working names and personal motivations in [characters](../game-design/characters.md).
- Equal racer stats initially.
- First vertical slice begins with a single-player capsule handling sandbox; split-screen is a later milestone.

## Still unresolved
- Protagonist selection/customization and roster size across game modes.
- Supporting cast identities and how the amateur enters organized competition.
- Championship unlocks, racing mechanics, local multiplayer count and final capture rules.
