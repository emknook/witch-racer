# Game design

## Core pitch
**Proposed:** An approachable, chaotic 3D broomstick racing game where eight distinct witches race through magical courses, alone, against AI, or in local split-screen.

## Confirmed requirements
- Godot, targeting the latest *stable* version after verifying compatibility.
- PSX-inspired low-poly assets made in Blender.
- Singleplayer and local multiplayer; keep architecture adaptable to future online play.
- Eight selectable witch racers for the foreseeable scope, including blue, yellow, green, red, purple and mixed hair colors.
- A simple, charming story rather than a complex narrative campaign.
- Readable, maintainable code and structured Markdown documentation.

## Proposed first playable vertical slice
One track; two selectable witches as early prototypes of the eventual eight; one shared movement model; checkpoint/lap timing; one-player time trial; two-player local split-screen. Expand to eight characters after the foundation works.

## Open design questions
- Race feel: arcade kart handling, free flight, or a hybrid constrained to a course?
- Items, boosts, drifting, and collisions?
- Camera perspective and 2–4-player performance targets?
- Is championship progression shared between solo and local play?
