# Story and setting

## Proposed premise: The Grand Broom Prix
Once a year, eight witches gather for a magical racing festival across enchanted regions. A mishap involving the ceremonial trophy destabilizes the courses, turning an ordinary competition into a delightful series of increasingly strange races. The racers continue because each has a personal reason to reach the finish.

## Narrative scope
Take inspiration from the *simplicity* of Overcooked's framing, not its specific plot. Tell the story through a short introduction, brief inter-cup interludes, environmental details and a short ending. Races remain the focus.

## Possible progression
1. **Invitation:** Introduce the festival and racer motivations.
2. **Opening cup:** Familiar flying lessons and friendly rivalry.
3. **Disruption:** Magical obstacles transform later courses.
4. **Final cup:** Racers overcome the festival's greatest challenge.
5. **Epilogue:** A short character-specific payoff for the selected witch.

## Open questions
- Is the magical mishap too elaborate for the desired tone?
- Is the tournament cooperative in spirit, fiercely competitive, or both?
- Are character endings unlockable, and does local multiplayer share story progression?

All story specifics remain proposals.
