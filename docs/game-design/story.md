# Story and setting

## Recovered Walpurgisnacht setting
Earlier project work established a richer premise: an ancient creature called Walpurgis escapes her prison, and witches race along magical routes while Wardens restore thirteen seals. See [Walpurgisnacht lore](walpurgisnacht.md) for recovered names and details.

This is now the **preferred working narrative direction** pending the owner's review. The Grand Broom Prix text below is retained only as a superseded early proposal, not as established canon.

## Superseded draft: Grand Broom Prix
Once a year, eight witches gather for a magical racing festival across enchanted regions. A mishap involving the ceremonial trophy destabilizes the courses, turning an ordinary competition into a delightful series of increasingly strange races. The racers continue because each has a personal reason to reach the finish.

## Narrative scope
Take inspiration from the *simplicity* of Overcooked's framing, not its specific plot. Short introduction, brief inter-cup interludes, environmental storytelling and a small ending. Racing remains the focus.
