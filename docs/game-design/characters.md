# Playable racers

**Confirmed:** The initial target roster contains **exactly eight selectable witches**. Hair colors include blue, yellow, green, red, purple and mixed-color styles. Names, appearances, motivations, and relationships below are **proposals**, not final canon.

| Working name | Hair | Visual hook | Motivation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Azure | Blue | Angular cape, sleek broom | Prove disciplined flying can beat flashy magic |
| Sunny | Yellow | Oversized goggles, bright scarf | Win a trophy for her small hometown |
| Moss | Green | Leaf-shaped hat, twig broom | Protect a beloved woodland race route |
| Ember | Red | Asymmetric coat, scorched broom | Break a legendary course record |
| Violet | Purple | Tall hat, crescent accessories | Uncover a mystery behind the tournament |
| Twilight | Blue–purple gradient | Star-patterned cloak | Test a broom she built herself |
| Blaze | Red–yellow streaks | Flame-like scarf | Finally beat a friendly rival |
| Prism | Multicolor | Patchwork outfit and charms | Show that unconventional magic belongs in racing |

## Design principles
- Silhouette and accessory differences must remain legible at low resolution and in split-screen.
- Hair color is an identifying cue, not the only distinction: ensure shape, value, outfit and icon contrast.
- **Proposed:** Keep racing stats equal initially; cosmetic selection avoids accidental competitive imbalance.
- Keep character definition separate from player input, camera, controller assignment and race state.
- Every witch gets one concise motivation and small optional story moments; no long dialogue requirement.
